Guest registration for small accommodation in Spain

For guest houses (pensiones), holiday flats and rural homes without property-management software: your guests fill in their details on their phone, you see at a glance which reports are missing, and you generate the file that SES.Hospedajes asks for. Free, open source and serverless.

Important: this is an unofficial tool. It does not send anything to the Ministry of the Interior: it prepares and validates the file, and you upload it to the SES.Hospedajes portal. The accommodation remains responsible for reporting each guest's details on time (24 hours) and correctly.

What it does

  • Guests fill in their form on their own phone, in Spanish, English, French, German, Italian or Portuguese, from a link or QR code you send them. Nothing to install, no accounts.
  • Reception imports it by scanning a QR code with the camera (or by pasting a code). You can also fill in the forms yourself.
  • Checks each report with clear messages (second surname missing, the DNI letter does not match…) and warns you about those due within 24 hours.
  • Generates the bulk-upload XML file (guest reports or accommodation bookings) following the Ministry's official document, and validates it twice before letting you download it.
  • Works offline once opened, and can be installed as an app on the reception computer, tablet or phone.

What it does NOT do

  • It does not send anything to the Ministry and does not connect to SES.Hospedajes. Until you upload the file to the portal, the report has not been made.
  • It does not take the responsibility off you: the accommodation answers for reporting on time and correctly. Check your first file on the portal: the app could not be tested against the live portal.
  • It does not store anything in the cloud or make backups for you: if you lose the device or the password without a backup, the data is lost.
  • It does not send email or SMS alerts: deadline warnings show up when you open the app.
  • It is not legal advice (GDPR, Spanish data-protection law): ask whoever is appropriate.

Privacy: everything stays on your device, encrypted

  • No server, no accounts, no analytics and no cookies: the data never leaves your device and nobody else can see it.
  • It is stored encrypted (AES-256) with a key derived from the establishment password. That password cannot be recovered: keep it safe.
  • The guest's form travels from their phone to reception as an encrypted code (QR, text or file), without going through any server.
  • Bookings are deleted automatically after 3 years (with prior warning), and you can download encrypted backups to keep outside the device.

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Open the app in the browser of the reception computer or tablet (at the address where you have published it; to try it out, in the demo) and create the establishment password: a first-steps wizard then guides you, with a test booking you can delete afterwards. If you like, install it from the browser to use it as an app, offline too.
  2. In the wizard (or later in Ajustes, Settings), enter the establishment details (the code SES.Hospedajes gave you) and create your first booking. Tap «Crear enlace para el huésped» (create guest link) and send it by WhatsApp or email.
  3. Scan the guest's QR code, review the report and, once it is complete, go to Exportar (Export), download the file and upload it yourself to the SES.Hospedajes portal within 24 hours.

And one habit: an encrypted backup once a week, kept outside that device.
